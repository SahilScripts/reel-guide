Privacy Policy

We collect only what is needed to deliver your session.

What we collect

  • Name and email you enter when booking (via Calendly) — used to send the Google Meet link and reminders.
  • Payment details are handled by Razorpay; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We receive only the payment status and amount.
  • Your Instagram username, if you message or comment to us.

How we use it

Only to schedule and run your session and to reply to you. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Third parties

Razorpay (payments), Calendly (booking), Google Meet (video call) and GitHub Pages (hosting this site) process data under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

Message us on Instagram to ask for your booking details to be deleted.
